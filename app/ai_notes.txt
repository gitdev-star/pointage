- In this company a person is identified by their badge number. attendance.user_id, v_daily.user_id and hr_all.employees_employee.employee_id (the numeric part, leading zeros ignored) are the same identifier. When a question says employee, employé, matricule, badge, personne or user, it means that number.
- Questions about presence, punches, lateness, overtime, absence or devices are answered from the attendance side only (v_daily, v_daily_summary, v_device_daily, attendance, v_anomalies), counted, grouped and filtered by user_id. Do not join the HR tables for them unless the question also asks for a name, section, department or factory.
- Questions about contract type, hire or termination date, status, job title, classification, leaves, schedules, shifts, departments, sections or factories are answered from the hr_all tables. Use the attendance side only if the question also mentions punches or lateness, and then join on the badge number.
- v_daily has one row per user per day. is_late and overtime_hours are already computed: never recompute them. The interface formats overtime as hours and minutes itself.
- v_employees lists everyone in the HR file, including people with no punches. v_daily_employee is v_daily with the HR identity added by a LEFT JOIN: its HR columns are empty for a user_id with no HR record, while user_id is always filled.
- In the attendance table use device_timestamp, not timestamp (timestamp is shifted by +3 hours before late August 2026).
- Today's date is given with each question: work out words like hier, ce mois, vendredi dernier yourself.

- A site is a clocker group: PBI1 (Itaosy), PBI2 (F12), PBI3 (F4-F7). For any question that mentions a site, PBI1, PBI2 or PBI3, or where people punched, use v_daily_site and filter on its site column with the short code, for example site = 'PBI2'. site_name holds the full label. Never compare a group name with a bare code.
- v_daily_site has one row per user, per day and per site, with full_name, first_punch, last_punch, arrival, departure, is_late and overtime_hours. "Who is present at site X on date D" is: SELECT DISTINCT user_id, full_name FROM v_daily_site WHERE date = D AND site = 'X'. Count people with count(DISTINCT user_id).
- When the question asks for people, a ranking or a list of employees, select full_name together with user_id from v_daily_site or v_daily_employee, never user_id alone.

- A person's classification (also called category, classe or grade) is in v_daily_class.classification, together with is_late, arrival, departure, overtime_hours and full_name. For any question that mixes lateness, presence or overtime with a classification or category, use v_daily_class.
- Compare classification case-insensitively: upper(classification) = 'M2'. For "except" or "not" a classification use: (classification IS NULL OR upper(classification) NOT IN ('OS1')). Several values: upper(classification) IN ('M2','OS1').
- Periods, from the date given with the question: this week = from the Monday of the current week to today (date_trunc('week', today)); last week = the previous Monday to Sunday; this month = from the 1st of the current month to today; last month = the whole previous month. Late means is_late = true, never recompute it.
- Example: "late people of classification M2 this week" -> SELECT user_id, full_name, date, arrival FROM v_daily_class WHERE is_late AND upper(classification) = 'M2' AND date >= date_trunc('week', DATE 'YYYY-MM-DD')::date AND date <= DATE 'YYYY-MM-DD' ORDER BY date, arrival LIMIT 200

- v_daily_class.classification_group is 'Cadres' (classification HC, hors catégorie) or 'Ouvriers' (every other classification); it is empty for people without a classification. Words cadre, cadres, manager, managers, management, executives, hors catégorie -> 'Cadres'. Words ouvrier, ouvriers, worker, workers, operator, operators, production staff -> 'Ouvriers'. Filter on classification_group, never on a list of codes, unless the user names a code such as M2 or OS1.
- "except cadres" or "not cadres": classification_group IS DISTINCT FROM 'Cadres'. "Only ouvriers": classification_group = 'Ouvriers'. For a count per group: GROUP BY classification_group.
- Example: "all late people except cadres this month" -> SELECT user_id, full_name, date, arrival, classification FROM v_daily_class WHERE is_late AND classification_group IS DISTINCT FROM 'Cadres' AND date >= date_trunc('month', DATE 'YYYY-MM-DD')::date AND date <= DATE 'YYYY-MM-DD' ORDER BY date, arrival LIMIT 200
